How it works

Simple from start to finish.

Design first, feedback next, development last. Every project follows this path, whichever package you pick.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You tell us what you need and share your logo if you have one. We agree scope and price.

  2. 02

    Design

    We design your sample page or pages, so you can see the direction before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Refine

    You give feedback and we polish it. The number of rounds depends on your package.

  4. 04

    Handover

    We build it, finalise it and hand over final files and a live site you own.

FAQ

Good questions.

Can't see yours? Ask us or chat on WhatsApp.

Which package should I choose?

Choose Basic if you already know what you want. Standard lets you approve one sample page first, then shape the rest as we build. Premium gives you several sample pages and a redesign before we build. Custom is fully managed and built around your needs. Not sure? Tell us your goals and we'll recommend the best fit. No pressure.

What do I need to give you to get started?

A short brief of what you need. For Basic we start from your logo, plus any brand colours you already use. Don't have a logo yet? Ask us about logo design.

How many changes can I ask for?
  • Basic: 1 round of feedback and corrections.
  • Standard: several changes are welcome while we build the full site.
  • Premium: we redesign from your feedback, and fine-tuning continues during development.
  • Custom: unlimited design feedback rounds, and building starts only after your full sign-off.
How long will it take?

It depends on the package and on how quickly feedback comes back. Typical turnaround: Basic [X–Y days], Standard [X–Y days], Premium [X–Y days]. For Custom, scope, timeline and price are agreed with you upfront.
